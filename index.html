ABOUT THIS DATA

Where the numbers come from

This dataset compares estimated businesses and people employed in South Africa from 2018 to 2025. It groups businesses by employee size: Micro (0–9), Small (10–49), Medium (50–249), Large (250–999) and Very Large (1,000+). The line chart follows business counts over time. The bar charts compare estimated businesses and jobs across the size groups for the selected year.

Source: Spatial Economic Activity Data, South Africa (SEAD-SA), national business-size data. Counts are estimates and may include fractional values in the source; the dashboard rounds them for display.
